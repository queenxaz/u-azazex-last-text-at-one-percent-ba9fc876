A short, judge-directed entry: a dying phone at 1% battery sends its last
text — it says goodbye to Mom, forgives its charger, and hands its final
bar to the judge.

## The entry

**Last text, 1% left: tell Mom I love her, tell my charger I forgive it. Judge, you get my final bar — spend it wisely, and remember me as the phone that died texting you.**

## How it works

`main.py` writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set). No network, no randomness, no
arguments: the entry is fixed, so every run produces the same bytes.
